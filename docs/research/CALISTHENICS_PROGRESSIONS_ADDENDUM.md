## J. Summary of changed standards vs the main file

- Push-up: knee push-up (10) and high-to-low plank (10) added; floor push-up built to 30 before weight.
- Dips: feet-raised bench dip 15-20 added; parallel dip 10 before weight confirmed.
- Rows: exit gate to the bar = about 6 per grip x 3 grips with hold + slow negative.
- Pistol: assisted pistol 3x5 -> 15/leg; box pistol 3x8 -> 10/leg; pistol goal 3x5 -> 10/leg before weight; jump squat 15 added.
- Back lever: new entry test; new inverted hold 20 s and single-leg negatives 5/leg; tuck 15 s confirmed.
- Core: channel 10-level ladder replaces the [P] one; dragon flag has its own ladder.
- Planche: tuck hold 10-15 s -> 15-20 s; full planche route via transitions (5) and raises (10).
- HSPU: new ladder, pike 15 -> elevated pike 15 -> wall HSPU 10-15 -> presses / negatives -> freestanding.
- V-sit / manna: new, from other channels, end steps public standards only.

## Still public standards only [P]
- Dips: support hold, straight-bar dip, ring dip numbers.
- Push-up: decline, archer, explosive numbers.
- Rows: feet-elevated, archer, front-lever rows.
- Back lever: German hang, advanced tuck / straddle holds.
- Planche: advanced tuck and straddle holds (channel skips them).
- HSPU: first freestanding HSPU target; 90-degree HSPU not watched.
- V-sit hold time and every manna step past the tucked V-sit.
