## 3. Content model

**Program categories (3):** Calisthenics, Weighted, Weighted calisthenics.

**Program lengths seen:** 7 days, 10 days, 14 days, 25 days, 28 days, 30 days, 1 week, 4 weeks, 5 weeks.
Most are 4 weeks. Two layouts:
- *By weeks*: week headers on a dotted timeline, day numbers run through the whole program
  (Day 1... Day 28), rest days shown as plain "REST DAY" text lines between cards.
- *By days*: a flat numbered list, no weeks (Strength Builder).

**Weekly shapes seen:**
- Skill programs (back lever, front lever, planche, muscle up, handstand, HSPU, human flag, L-sit):
  4 sessions a week, pattern skill / skill + core / skill / support strength, rest days between,
  and the last week is a **"(CHALLENGE)"** week. All sessions labelled 60 mins.
- Muscle programs: 5-6 sessions a week body-part split (legs, chest & triceps, back & biceps,
  core, shoulders) or push/pull/arms. 45-60 mins.
- Each card shows only day number, name and minutes. No sets, exercise count or difficulty on the card.

**Program header stats:** length (weeks or days) and number of workouts. No level, equipment or
place shown on the detail page *(the place is only known through the Training Spot filter)*.

**Workout (day) detail:**
- Name, duration tag, categories, header photo.
- Settings card: Difficulty (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced), Warmup on/off, Auto reps on/off.
  *Auto reps meaning not shown; probably the app counts or advances reps by itself. Unsure.*
- Exercises grouped into **rounds** ("ROUND 1 - 4 sets"). Each row: photo, name, one target
  (reps, "reps each", time in seconds, or "max reps") and its own rest time.
- Rest time differs by level (45 s at Intermediate, 60 s at Beginner and Advanced in the one sample).
- **Difficulty swaps whole exercises** (Beginner diamond *knee* push-ups → Intermediate push-ups →
  Advanced *archer* push-ups) and changes reps. So one workout holds three versions.
- "Target Muscles": front/back body drawing with worked muscles in red and a text label.
- Buttons: SCHEDULE (put it on the calendar) and START WORKOUT.

**Exercise library and exercise detail page:** *not seen.* Exercises appear only as rows. Names
carry the equipment as a suffix ("- Weighted Vest"). No tap into an exercise was recorded, so
video, cues, alternatives and progressions on that page are unknown.

**Skills / progressions:** handled as whole programs ("Complete X Program For All Levels"), not as
a skill tree or level ladder inside the app. Inside one workout, the only ladder is the
3-step difficulty switch.

**Levels:** Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced, used in the difficulty switch and in the Create
program form.

## 8. Strong ideas worth adapting

1. **Difficulty switch that swaps exercises**, not just reps: one workout, three versions
   (knee push-up / push-up / archer push-up). Simple control, real progression.
2. **Warm-up as a switch** on the workout page, so the workout itself stays short.
3. **Training Spot tiles** (Home, Gym, Park, Anywhere) as a first filter: plain words people know.
4. **Skill programs with a fixed rhythm** (skill / skill + core / skill / support) and a final
   **Challenge week**. Easy to understand and to build.
5. **Program timeline** with week headers, numbered day cards, minutes, and rest days drawn as
   text between cards. You see the whole month at a glance.
6. **Program header with two stats only** (length, number of workouts). Clean.
7. **Target muscles drawing** on each workout with the worked area in red.
8. **Per-exercise rest time** shown right on the row ("12 reps - Rest 45 seconds").
9. "Create your own" offered right where a search fails ("Couldn't find a program?").
10. Empty states with one clear action (No workout history → Explore activities).

## 9. Weak points / gaps a better app would fix

1. **Dead-end filter:** Home Gym gives "No programs found" with no suggestion.
2. **Almost everything behind the paywall:** only Day 1 free, sometimes not even that. A user
   cannot judge a program before paying.
3. **Program cards hide key facts:** no level, equipment, place or days-per-week on the card or
   the detail header. You must open each workout to learn the equipment.
4. **Every skill program looks the same** (four 60-minute days with the same names every week).
   No visible progression from week to week; "60 mins" on every card looks like a placeholder.
5. **Skills are separate programs, not a ladder.** No "you are here" level test, no step-by-step
   skill path (e.g. tuck → advanced tuck → straddle → full).
6. **Three ways to save** (bookmark, heart, collections) with no clear difference.
7. **Profile is empty for new users** and stats are Pro-only, so there is no early reward.
8. Small polish faults: the Description box says "Add a name...", rest days drawn twice in a row
   without dates, long titles cut short in the top bar, grey placeholders while photos load.
9. Difficulty choice is per workout and resets? *(Unsure if it is saved for the whole program.)*
10. No clear onboarding link: "Recommended for you" gives no reason why.
