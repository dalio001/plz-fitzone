# Calisthenics skill progressions - research reference for PLZ Train

Compiled 2026-11-06. Purpose: training knowledge (steps, order, readiness standards) for designing
our OWN calisthenics mode. All wording below is our own paraphrase; nothing is copied from a creator
or app. Numbers are training standards, not anyone's content.

Sources

  - V8 How To Muscle Up - The Best Way - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_iYvlSMgUGE
- [P] = widely used public progression standards (common bodyweight-fitness ladders: beginner
  routines with "3x8 to 3x12 then move on", static-hold ladders tuck -> adv tuck -> straddle -> full).
  Used to cross-check and to fill skills the videos did not cover (dips, rows, squat/pistol,
  back lever, core). Not taken from any single proprietary source.

Conventions
- Type: `reps` or `hold` (seconds).
- "Ready" = the standard to unlock the next step. Default rule when unsure: 3 sets x 8-12 clean reps,
  or 3 x 20-30 s clean hold. Video numbers are single-set "consecutive with perfect form"; we
  translate them to sets where helpful and mark the original.
- Equipment: floor, wall, bar (pull-up bar), low bar (hip height bar / rings / table edge),
  dip bars (parallel bars), parallettes (or two stable boxes/chairs), rings, pole (vertical bar), box.

## 1. Push-up (floor push) - to planche push-up
Sources: V2 (main), P.

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Incline push-up (hands on raised surface; lower the surface over time) | reps | 3x10 at roughly knee height (V2: 10 clean reps) | box/bench/wall |
| 2 | Floor push-up | reps | 3x10-15 (V2: 10 clean) | floor |
| 3 | Feet-elevated (decline) push-up | reps | 3x10 (V2: 10), raise feet higher over time | box |
| 4 | Archer push-up | reps | 3x6 each side | floor |
| 5 | Explosive push-up, then clap push-up | reps | 20 clap push-ups in a row (V2) / 3x8 [P] | floor |
| 6 | Pseudo-planche push-up (hands at waist, lean forward) | reps | 12 clean in a row (V2) | floor / wall at feet |
| 7 | 90-degree push-up (pseudo push-up that ends in a short tuck/bent-arm hold) | reps | 10 clean in a row (V2) | floor |
| 8 | Planche push-up from the ground, then full planche push-up (goal) | reps | 10 lifts from the floor without kicking (V2), then 1+ full | floor / parallettes |

Form cues (our words)
- Body is one straight plank from heels to shoulders; squeeze belly and glutes.
- Hands under the chest line, elbows tucked about 45 degrees, chest goes all the way down.
- Incline height is the dial: higher = easier. Use it to learn perfect form before the floor.
- From step 6 on, hands stay at the waistline at the top AND bottom of every rep; lean so the weight
  goes past the hands. If hands creep back to chest level, the rep does not count.

## 2. Pull-up (vertical pull) - to one-arm pull-up
Sources: V1 (main), P.

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 0 | Dead hang / active hang (shoulder blades pulled down) [P] | hold | 3x30 s | bar |
| 1 | Australian (inverted) row, body at hip-height angle | reps | 10 clean at hip height (V1) / 3x10 | low bar / rings / dip bars |
| 2 | Jumping negative pull-up (jump up, lower slowly) | reps | 12 clean in a row (V1); aim for 3-5 s lowering | bar |
| 3 | Pull-up (all grips: overhand, chin-up, mixed) | reps | 10 clean in a row (V1) / 3x8 [P] | bar |
| 4 | L-sit pull-up (start tucked if needed) | reps | 6 in a row (V1) | bar |
| 5 | Archer pull-up (one arm does most of the work) | reps | 8 in a row (V1); can start with archer rows | bar |
| 6 | High / chest-to-bar pull-up (explosive, bar to belly) | reps | 5 in a row (V1) | bar |
| 7 | One-arm negative | reps | 6 per arm in a row (V1) | bar |
| 8 | One-arm pull-up (goal) | reps | 1, then build to 4-5 per arm before adding weight (V1) | bar |

Form cues
- Grip hard, shoulder blades squeezed back and down before pulling.
- Whole body tight as one unit: legs together, toes pointed, no swing.
- Lean back slightly and aim the chest at the bar; chin over is the minimum, chest is the goal.
- Lower under control every rep; negatives build the strength for the full rep.
- Row: the lower (more horizontal) the body, the harder. Bar to chest, straight body.

Note: V1 also lists "inverted row pull-up" (row from a skin-the-cat / toes-to-bar position) between
L-sit and archer. It is advanced and bar-skill heavy; optional in our ladder.

## 3. Dip (vertical push) - to straight-bar dip / weighted / ring dip
Sources: P (cross-checked with V8, which uses 15 straight-bar dips and 10 dips as muscle-up entry).

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Bench dip (hands behind on bench, feet on floor, knees bent -> straight) | reps | 3x15 | bench/chair |
| 2 | Support hold (top of dip, arms locked) | hold | 3x30 s | dip bars |
| 3 | Negative dip (5 s down) | reps | 3x5 | dip bars |
| 4 | Parallel-bar dip | reps | 3x10 (single set of 15 is a common muscle-up prerequisite) | dip bars |
| 5 | Straight-bar dip | reps | 3x10 / 15 in a row (V8 prerequisite) | bar |
| 6 | Ring support hold, rings turned out | hold | 3x30 s | rings |
| 7 | Ring dip (goal) / weighted dip | reps | 3x8 | rings / dip bars |

Form cues
- Shoulders down away from ears at the top; lock the elbows.
- Go down until the shoulder is level with or just below the elbow; no deeper while learning.
- Slight forward lean; elbows go back, not flaring out.
- Straight-bar dip: lean over the bar, bar near the belly at the bottom.

## 4. Horizontal row - to front-lever row
Sources: P (V1 step 1 and V4 confirm row and tuck-lever holds).

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Vertical row (body close to upright) | reps | 3x12 | low bar / rings / table |
| 2 | Incline row (body about 45 degrees) | reps | 3x10 | low bar / rings |
| 3 | Horizontal row (body parallel to floor, feet on floor) | reps | 3x10 | low bar / rings |
| 4 | Feet-elevated row | reps | 3x10 | rings + box |
| 5 | Archer row / one-arm-assisted row | reps | 3x6 each side | rings / low bar |
| 6 | Tuck front-lever row | reps | 3x6 | bar / rings |
| 7 | Advanced tuck / straddle front-lever row (goal) | reps | 3x5 | bar / rings |

Form cues
- Straight body (no sagging hips), pull the bar to the lower chest, squeeze shoulder blades.
- Pause 1 s at the top, lower slowly.

## 5. Squat - to pistol squat (one-leg squat)
Sources: P (channel has leg workouts but no clear pistol progression video).

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Assisted squat (hold a door frame / pole) | reps | 3x15 | pole/door |
| 2 | Full bodyweight squat (hips below knees) | reps | 3x20 | floor |
| 3 | Split squat, then rear-foot-elevated (Bulgarian) split squat | reps | 3x10 each leg | floor / box |
| 4 | Step-up / Cossack squat (side squat) | reps | 3x8 each side | box / floor |
| 5 | Box pistol (sit to a box on one leg, stand up), lower the box over time | reps | 3x8 each leg to a low box | box |
| 6 | Assisted pistol (hold a pole or band) / pistol negative | reps | 3x5 each leg | pole |
| 7 | Pistol squat (goal), then shrimp squat as a parallel goal | reps | 3x5 each leg | floor |

Form cues
- Heel stays down, knee tracks over the toes, chest up.
- Free leg reaches forward and straight (pistol) - needs hamstring and hip-flexor mobility.
- Counterweight (arms forward, or holding a light weight in front) makes balance easier.

## 6. L-sit (compression core + straight-arm support)
Sources: V3 (main), P.

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 0 | Support hold, shoulders pushed down [P] | hold | 3x30 s | parallettes / dip bars / two boxes |
| 1 | Tuck L-sit hold | hold | 20-30 s (V3: start 5 s, build to 20-30 s) | parallettes / boxes |
| 2 | L-sit kicks (from tuck, push legs out as far as possible, return) | reps | 10 in a row or 20 s of continuous kicks (V3) | parallettes |
| 3 | Alternating single-leg L-sit | hold | 20 s continuous, slowing the switch (V3) | parallettes |
| 4 | L-sit walks on the floor (slow scoot forward/back) | reps | slow, controlled sets; feeds into the hold (V3) | floor |
| 5 | L-sit hold on raised surface | hold | 3x10-15 s [P] | parallettes |
| 6 | L-sit hold on the floor (goal) | hold | 3x15-20 s [P] | floor |
| 7 | (Next skill) V-sit / hanging L-sit | hold | 10 s | floor / bar |

Form cues
- Arms locked, press palms down hard, shoulders pushed away from ears.
- Legs straight, together, toes pointed; legs at hip height (90 degrees).
- Raised surfaces give leg clearance - use them first, move to the floor later.
- Train mobility alongside: standing toe touch with crossed legs, seated forward fold reaching for
  ankles; breathe out to sink deeper (V3).

## 7. Handstand hold
Sources: V5 (main), P.

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Pike hold (hips high, straight line hands-hips and hips-heels) | hold | 3x30 s [P]; until comfortable (V5) | floor |
| 2 | Elevated (feet-on-box) pike hold | hold | 3x30 s | box |
| 3 | Wall walk to chest-to-wall handstand | reps + hold | 3 wall walks in a row and 20 s hold at the top (V5) | wall |
| 4 | Kick-up to handstand (against the wall, then near it) | reps | consistent controlled kick-ups, about 8/10 landing softly [P] | wall |
| 5 | Fingertip press off the wall (learn to steer back) | reps | 15 in a row (V5) | wall |
| 6 | Press-and-release balance near the wall (feet a hand-width off) | hold | 20-30 s without touching (V5) | wall |
| 7 | Dismount / bail (turn the hips and step down) | reps | comfortable both sides (V5) | floor |
| 8 | Freestanding handstand (goal) | hold | 3x30 s [P] | floor |

Form cues
- Stacked line: hands - shoulders - hips - feet. Push the floor tall, shoulders by the ears.
- Arms locked the entire time; a bending arm means the fall has started.
- Fingers spread wide; balance is a constant small press (fingertips = come back) and release
  (palm = go forward).
- Kick up leading with the hips, same effort each time; "fall into place", do not slam the wall.
- Watch the hips: avoid a banana arch; ribs in, glutes tight.
- Chest-to-wall (wall walk) teaches the straight line better than back-to-wall.
- Always learn the bail (cartwheel step-out) before going free.

## 8. Muscle-up (bar)
Sources: V8 (main), V1 (high pull-up), P.

Prerequisites (V8 test): 15 pull-ups, 15 s hanging L-sit, 15 straight-bar dips, 10 dips,
hanging leg raises, negative muscle-ups, jumping muscle-ups.

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Strength base: pull-ups + straight-bar dips | reps | 3x10 pull-ups and 3x10 straight-bar dips [P]; V8 asks 15 each | bar |
| 2 | Chest-to-bar / high pull-up (explosive) | reps | 3x5 bar to chest or lower [P, V1] | bar |
| 3 | Swing rhythm ("step down" into the swing: hollow-arch beat with legs) | reps | 10 smooth swings in a row (V8) | bar |
| 4 | Swing to L-sit pull-back (snap legs to L at the back of the swing) | reps | 5-10 controlled in a row (V8) | bar |
| 5 | Negative muscle-up (from top of bar, lower slowly through the transition) | reps | 3x3 slow negatives [P] | bar / low bar |
| 6 | Jumping muscle-up / band-assisted muscle-up | reps | 3x3 [P] | low bar / band |
| 7 | Lay-over-the-bar (pull around the bar and fold over it, then push up) | reps | 3 in a row (V8) | bar |
| 8 | Strict-ish bar muscle-up (goal) | reps | 1, then 3x3, then 5+ in a row | bar |

Form cues
- Grip about shoulder width, shoulder blades active (not a passive dead hang).
- Use the swing: legs snap into an L at the back of the swing and stop sharply - that stop helps
  throw the body up.
- Pull in a curve AROUND the bar ("C" path), not straight up into it; pull the bar toward the hips.
- Keep legs tight and up through the pull; dropping them early kills the height.
- Lean forward over the bar, then press out like a straight-bar dip.

## 9. Front lever
Sources: V4 (main), P.

Prerequisites (V4): 10-15 pull-ups, 20 s hanging L-sit, 15 hanging leg raises, 5-10 L-sit pull-ups.

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Toes-to-bar (lean back, lats on) | reps | 10 clean in a row (V4) | bar |
| 2 | Tuck front lever | hold | 15 s clean (V4) / 3x10-15 s [P] | bar |
| 3 | Advanced tuck front lever (back flat, knees at 90) [P; V4 shows "open the tuck"] | hold | 3x10-15 s | bar |
| 4 | Inverted hang to "upside-down deadlift" (hips drive up along the bar into a straight inverted line) | reps | 10 clean in a row (V4) | bar |
| 5 | Front-lever negatives / drops (single leg first, then both) | reps | 6 per leg, then 10 with both legs (V4) | bar |
| 6 | Straddle / one-leg front lever hold [P] | hold | 3x8-10 s | bar |
| 7 | Front-lever raises (single leg first) - train after drops | reps | 6-7 per leg, then both legs (V4) | bar |
| 8 | Full front lever (goal) | hold | 3x10 s [P] | bar / rings |

Form cues
- Arms fully locked; push the bar toward the hips (straight-arm lat pull-down feeling).
- Body completely straight - even a small hip bend means the lower back and glutes are off.
- Lean back on the shoulder blades and lift from the lower back/hips, not by lifting the feet.
- Keep breathing; look just past the bar, do not throw the head.
- Tighter tuck = easier; open the tuck as it gets easy.
- Bonus builder: "ice-cream makers" (top of pull-up -> push the bar away into lever line -> back).

## 10. Back lever
Sources: P (channel has only a demo video, no tutorial was found).

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 0 | Prereqs: 8-10 pull-ups, 10 dips, German hang comfortable [P] | - | - | - |
| 1 | Skin the cat (slow, to German hang and back) | reps | 3x5 slow | rings / bar |
| 2 | German hang | hold | 3x20-30 s | rings / bar |
| 3 | Tuck back lever | hold | 3x15 s | rings / bar |
| 4 | Advanced tuck back lever (back flat) | hold | 3x10-15 s | rings / bar |
| 5 | One-leg / straddle back lever | hold | 3x10 s | rings / bar |
| 6 | Half lay (hips straight, knees bent) / back-lever negatives | hold | 3x8-10 s | rings |
| 7 | Full back lever (goal) | hold | 3x10 s | rings / bar |

Form cues
- Arms locked; biceps tendon is under load - progress slowly, start every session with German hang
  for shoulder prep.
- Hips open (straight line), glutes squeezed, body facing the floor.
- Rings are kinder to elbows/shoulders than a bar.

## 11. Planche
Sources: V6 (main, up to tuck planche), V2 (pseudo push-ups, waist hands), P (beyond tuck).

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Scapula push-up (arms locked, only shoulder blades move; thumbs forward) | reps | 20 clean (V6) | floor |
| 2 | Straight-arm support shrug (dip-bar press down) + 5 s hold at top | reps | 15 + 5 s hold (V6) | dip bars / parallettes |
| 3 | Planche lean with protraction shrugs (lean until hands near waist) | reps / hold | 15 + 5 s hold (V6); [P] lean hold 3x20-30 s | floor |
| 4 | Tuck L-sit swing (rock forward toward tuck planche, controlled) | reps | 12 controlled (V6) | parallettes |
| 5 | Raise to tuck planche (lean forward, lift hips to horizontal) | reps | 8 (V6) | parallettes |
| 6 | Straight-arm light-dumbbell front raise negatives + hold (helper for straight-arm strength) | reps | 8 + 5 s hold (V6); start ~2 kg | dumbbells |
| 7 | Tuck planche toe taps (1 s holds between taps) | reps | 8 with 1 s between (V6) | floor / parallettes |
| 8 | Tuck planche hold | hold | 10-15 s (V6) -> then 3x10-15 s [P] | parallettes / floor |
| 9 | Advanced tuck -> straddle -> full planche (long-term goal) [P] | hold | 3x10-15 s each before the next | parallettes |

Form cues
- Arms LOCKED (turn the elbow pits forward), shoulder blades pushed apart (rounded upper back).
- Hands at the waistline; lean forward to bring the shoulders well past the hands.
- Hips up to shoulder height, back flat in the tuck, toes pointed, look slightly forward.
- Wrists stacked over the grip; warm wrists well. Very slow skill: months to years.

## 12. Human flag
Sources: V7 (main), P.

Helpful prep (V7): scapula shrugs, planche leans, side plank, archer push-ups, archer pull-ups,
hanging windshield wipers.

| # | Step | Type | Ready for next | Equipment |
|---|------|------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | High side plank raises (straight arm, hip up/down, top hand overhead) | reps | 10 each side, effortless (V7); harder: feet raised | floor |
| 2 | Side-plank flag (bottom hand on floor by the pole, top hand on pole, lift legs) | reps | 10 each side (V7) | pole + floor |
| 3 | Vertical flag / support hold on pole (both arms straight, body vertical) [P] | hold | 3x10 s each side | pole |
| 4 | Straddle flag raises (hop up, legs wide) | reps | 5 each side (V7) | pole |
| 5 | Tuck or straddle flag hold [P] | hold | 3x5-10 s each side | pole |
| 6 | Flag raises (straddle up, close legs, hold, lower) | reps | 5 each side, holding longer each time (V7) | pole |
| 7 | Full human flag (goal) | hold | 3x5-10 s each side [P] | pole |

Form cues
- Top arm pulls, bottom arm pushes - both straight.
- Bottom hand low (about knee height), thumb down; top hand wide - narrower is harder.
- Head between the arms; the flag happens directly in line with the pole, not in front or behind.
- Side core (obliques) and lats do the lifting; squeeze legs together for the final hold.

## How the creator structures training (our summary)

- Find your level: test the ladder from the top, start at the hardest step you can do with clean
  form. Every higher step is earned only by meeting the stated standard.
- Train the step you are on PLUS the easier steps below it. Typical "drop set" down the ladder:
  max clean reps at your step, then immediately the next-easier step, and so on down to the easiest,
  so the muscles get more total work without breaking form.
- Every Minute On the Minute (EMOM) for new skills: if you can only do 1 clean rep or a short hold,
  do 1 at the start of every minute for 10-20 minutes (stop early when form breaks). When it gets
  easy, do 2 per minute, then 3, and so on. Then regress to the next-easier step and EMOM that.
- Form first, then volume: only add reps once a rep is perfect. Negatives (slow lowering) are the
  bridge when the full rep is not there yet.
- Prerequisite gates before advanced skills (front lever, muscle-up): a strength test must be
  passed so the session is about technique, not survival.
- Order inside a session (implied by the videos and standard practice): warm-up (wrists, shoulders,
  mobility for the skill) -> skill work while fresh (holds, balance, technique, EMOM) -> strength
  ladder work (reps, drop sets) -> conditioning / core finisher.
- Rest: skill holds and heavy steps get long rest (about 1-3 min, or the remainder of the EMOM minute);
  lighter rep work 60-90 s. Stop a skill set when form breaks.
- Frequency [P]: 3 full-body sessions a week for beginners (push, pull, legs, core each time);
  skills like handstand can be practiced in short daily blocks (5-10 min) because they are mostly
  balance; heavy straight-arm skills (planche, levers) 2-3x a week with a rest day between.
- Load progression after the goal: more reps, then added weight (vest/belt), then harder variation.
- Patience notes: front lever months to a year; planche longer. Straight-arm work stresses elbows
  and wrists - progress gradually.

## Ideas for our mode (not from the videos)
- Each skill = a ladder of 6-8 steps; each step stores {type, target, sets, equipment, cues[]}.
- Unlock rule: hit the target on 2 separate sessions before offering the next step.
- Built-in EMOM timer and a "drop down the ladder" auto-finisher.
- Equipment filter (floor only / bar / dip bars / rings / pole) hides skills you cannot train.
