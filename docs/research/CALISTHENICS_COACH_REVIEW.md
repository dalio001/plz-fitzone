# Calisthenics coach review of the ladders (P2-20, 2026-11-09)

Owner: "the progression a little bit not accurate... act as a calisthenics coach... more specific,
more accurate, more personalized". Every ladder was checked against the Chris Heria notes already in
CALISTHENICS_PROGRESSIONS*.md (primary) and cross-checked with:
- Overcoming Gravity 2 level charts (Steven Low), read in full:
  https://www.calisthenics-101.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Overcoming-Gravity-2nd-Edition-Exercise-Charts.pdf
- r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine (copies): https://gist.github.com/fi0/d13d851347c8e29e1a3e0ec7042fe149
- Antranik static-hold progressions: https://antranik.org/?p=6191
- Muscle-up standards: barbend.com/steps-for-muscle-up, crossfit.com/pro-coach/developing-a-muscle-up
Not reached directly: FitnessFAQs, GMB, Saturno, Hybrid Calisthenics (search summaries only).

## What changed (ladder by ladder)
- Push: clap push-up is a power drill (not the step before the pseudo planche push-up). Archer needs 20
  push-ups. Planche push-up needs straddle planche 8 s.
- Pull: archer needs 12 pull-ups; one-arm negative needs 15 pull-ups + 5 archers + 15 s one-arm hang.
- Dip: support hold right after bench dips and before negatives (needs 30 s); ring dip needs ring
  support 30 s + 8 dips; weighted dip after 15 dips.
- Legs: split squat added before the Bulgarian; jump squat is a drill; beginner shrimp before the pistol.
- Core: hollow rock before the V-up. Hanging core: wipers need 10 toes-to-bar; around-the-world per side.
- L-sit: tuck 20-30 s, L-sit 15-30 s, straddle L-sit added (bridge to the V-sit); manna marked elite.
- Handstand: bail learned before kicking up; chest-to-wall 30-60 s; kick-up needs 30 s on the wall;
  freestanding needs bail + 45 s wall + 20 s wall balance. Toe pulls, shoulder taps, press negative
  are drills.
- Handstand push-up: wall HSPU negative added before the wall HSPU; deficit wall HSPU added after;
  freestanding negatives need a 20 s freestanding handstand, the full rep 30 s. Balance drills moved out.
- Muscle-up: lock was too high (20 pull-ups, 20 bar dips, floor L-sit). Now 12 pull-ups, 5 chest-to-bar,
  15 bar dips, 15 s hanging L-sit (Heria). Low-bar transition drill added before negatives.
- Front lever: upside-down deadlift and negatives are drills next to the tucks; one-leg needs adv tuck 10 s.
- Back lever: reordered inverted hang, German hang, skin the cat, tuck, advanced tuck, straddle,
  one-leg (new), full; levels lowered (it is easier than the front lever); negatives are drills.
- Planche: frog stand added first; reverse leg raise and L-sit-to-tuck are drills; half-lay added
  between straddle and full; lock = 15 s L-sit, 20 push-ups, 10 dips.
- Human flag: BUG fixed, the lock pointed at a step inside its own ladder so it never opened. Now
  10 pull-ups + 10 dips. Vertical flag and advanced tuck flag added; tuck before straddle.
- Dragon flag: lock on core (30 s hollow hold, 15 lying leg raises); tuck hold added before negatives;
  full needs 5 slow negatives.
- Mobility: knee-to-wall ankle stretch added (pistol).

## Coaching rules added
- Drills: done next to your step on a skill day, listed under "Coach drills" on each ladder; never
  a step you must pass.
- Personal pace: 3 clean sessions to move up (instead of 2) when a joint is marked sore or the profile
  age is 40+; the joint's warm-up joins every skill block; straight-arm skills at most twice a week.
- Aims refresh after every finished workout (the per-set aim already followed your last sets).
- Saved places move by exercise, so nobody loses their step when the order changes.
