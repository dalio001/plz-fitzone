# Mobility library (P2-21, 2026-11-11)

Owner request: mobility as its own sub-category, by body part, with pictures.

## How it is built
- 10 areas, each 4–9 moves, easy (Foundation) first. No ladder and nothing to pass: mobility is done
  the same day, slowly, every day if wanted. Holds 20–60 s, controlled circles (CARs) 3–5 slow reps.
- Full-body routine = the first easy move of every area your gear allows (~15 min).
- Gear-aware like the ladders (wall, bench/chair, box/step, band); missing gear greys the move out.

## Sources (cross-checked; own wording in the app)
- Chris Heria / THENX warm-up and flexibility routines (wrist prep, shoulder pass, pike, bridge, pancake).
- r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine warm-up and the Molding Mobility / Starting Stretching routines
  (wrist, shoulder, hip, hamstring stretches; hold times 30–60 s).
- Antranik "Beginner flexibility routine" and Tom Merrick "Bodyweight Warrior" (puppy stretch, frog,
  pigeon, Jefferson curl, couch stretch, bridge progression).
- Functional Range Conditioning controlled articular rotations (neck, shoulder, hip, ankle circles).
- Knees Over Toes / Ben Patrick (tibialis raise, Peterson step-up, knee-over-toe split squat, heel sit).
- GMB / FitnessFAQs wrist prep (fingers-back, back-of-hand, wrist push-ups).

## Areas
neck, shoulders, wrists & forearms, upper back, lower back & spine, hips, hamstrings,
hip flexors & quads, knees, ankles & calves. The full list with sets lives in `CALI_MOBILITY` (index.html).
