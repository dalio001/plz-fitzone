## 2. Final gear list

Verdict on the proposed list: keep it, with three changes.
- **Add "broomstick + 2 chairs"** as a named setup. Heria uses it in `4Bn3ERXEK-k`.
- **Add "fixed vertical anchor"**: something solid to grip behind the head for the dragon flag.
- **Add "stack of books"** as a stand-in for parallettes (`Qcde45O8lzo`).
- **Keep "door + towel" but flag it [general practice] and give it the lowest priority.** No source video uses it, and it carries the most risk.
- Light dumbbells are optional. Heria uses them for straight-arm flies (planche) and weighted pistols. Not needed for the ladders.

| Gear | Definition | Safety note |
|---|---|---|
| floor | Flat, non-slip floor space about 2 m x 1 m | Mat for knees and back. Clear the space around you for clap push-ups and jumps |
| wall | Clear, solid wall | Handstands only against a load-bearing wall with nothing hanging on it. Clear the floor below |
| box/step | Sturdy box, stair step or low platform, 20-60 cm | Must not slide. Stairs: use the bottom step only |
| bench | Flat bench or heavy couch edge | Couch: only the firm front frame, never the cushion |
| 2 sturdy chairs | Two identical solid chairs (4 legs, no wheels, not folding) | Must not tip or slide: put them on a rug-free floor, back them against a wall or furniture, and load straight down over the legs. Never lean the load toward the chair's edge. Test with a slow half load first |
| broomstick + 2 chairs | Broom or pipe resting across two chair seats or backs as a low bar | Heria's setup. Only for rows with the feet on the floor. Use a solid wooden or steel pole; a hollow plastic broom can snap. Weight the chairs (someone sitting on them, or heavy books on the seats). Keep reps slow; no jerking |
| sturdy table | Solid table (wood or metal frame) for rows underneath [general practice] | Must hold your whole body weight on one edge without lifting the other end. No glass, no folding tables, no pedestal tables. Grip the long side near a leg. Test with a slow half-weight pull |
| door + towel | Towel knotted and shut in a door, or wrapped around both handles, for rows [general practice] | Only with a door that closes TOWARD you as you pull (you pull against the frame, not the latch). Lock it, or have someone hold the far side. Never pull on a door that opens toward you. Check the handle fixings. Lowest priority |
| pull-up bar | Bar high enough to hang with feet clear: fixed, door-frame or park bar | Door-frame bars only on a solid frame. No kipping or muscle-ups on door-frame bars |
| low bar | Bar at about hip height: park bar, Smith rack, dip-bar side, or the broomstick setup | Must be fixed. Check that it does not roll |
| dip bars | Parallel bars about shoulder-width apart, high enough for full depth | Floor dip stations must not tip: check the base width |
| parallettes | Low parallel handles 10-30 cm high | Homemade PVC ones slide on smooth floors (`yehdZfvOOkE`); use them on a mat or rug-free grippy floor |
| stack of books | Two even, equal stacks of hardcover books, a few cm high | Only for L-sit or tuck holds with straight downward pressure. Not for planche swings or jumps. Flat, equal stacks only |
| rings | Gymnastic rings on straps from a bar, beam or branch | Anchor rated for body weight. Straps checked every session |
| pole / wall bars | Vertical pole, or wall bars bolted to a wall | For the flag: must be anchored at top and bottom, or set in concrete. A street sign only if it is solid steel and does not flex |
| fixed vertical anchor | Something solid to grip behind the head when lying on the floor: pole, wall bars, heavy fixed furniture leg | For the dragon flag. Must not slide or tip when pulled toward your head. Never a chair |
| resistance band | Loop bands in several thicknesses | Check for nicks before each use. Loop it tightly on the bar so it cannot slide. Keep your face out of the line of the band |
| weight vest / backpack | Vest, weight belt, or a backpack of books | Backpack: straps tight, weight high and close to the back, nothing hard pressing on the spine. Only after the weighted requirements (section 5) |

## 3. Alternatives per ladder step (when the listed gear is missing)

Format per alternative: **name** (gear), difficulty vs the original, setup / movement / common mistake. Alternatives are listed in priority order.

### Push

**Incline Push-Up [box]**
1. **Couch / Chair / Stair Push-Up** (bench, chair or stair), *same*: hands on a stable edge, body straight / lower your chest to the edge, press back up / hips sagging. (`mcxtFRpZF3I`, `J7R8whSA17Q`: higher surface = easier.)
2. **Wall Push-Up** (wall), *easier*: hands on the wall at chest height, feet back / bend your elbows until your nose nears the wall / feet too close, so it does nothing.
3. **Table-Edge Push-Up** (sturdy table), *easier*: hands on the table edge / same motion / table that slides away.

**Decline Push-Up [box]**
1. **Chair Decline Push-Up** (chair against a wall), *same*: feet on the seat, hands on the floor / full push-up / chair sliding back (put it against a wall). (`J7R8whSA17Q`)
2. **Stair / Couch Decline** (bottom stair or couch), *same or easier, depending on height*: feet up, hands on the floor / push-up / hips piking up.

**Tuck Planche Push-Up [parallettes]**
1. **Two-Chair Tuck Planche Push-Up** (2 chairs, same height), *same*: one hand on each seat edge, chairs braced / lean into the tuck, bend and press / chairs creeping apart. (Heria uses chairs for planche work in `OmKfROtB45Q`.)
2. **Floor Tuck Planche Push-Up** (floor), *harder*: flat hands, fingers turned out / same / wrists unwarmed; less depth. (`OmKfROtB45Q`: flat hands are harder than parallettes.)
3. **Band-Assisted Tuck Planche Push-Up** (band + high anchor + floor), *easier*: see section 4.

**Planche Push-Up [parallettes]**: same order as above (two chairs = same, floor = harder, band = easier).

### Pull (whole ladder needs a hang point)

No household item gives a true vertical pull. Without a hang point, the honest answer is to train the row ladder and mark the pull ladder "paused".

**Active Hang / Scapula Pull-Up / Negative Pull-Up / Pull-Up / L-Sit Pull-Up / Chest-to-Bar / Archer / One-Arm Negative / One-Arm Pull-Up [bar]**
1. **Rings from a beam or branch** (rings), *same; the rings are less stable, so a little harder.* All steps can be done on rings. (`FxkULisPEo4` does ring L-sit pull-ups.)
2. **Park or playground bar, sturdy tree branch** (outdoor), *same*: check that the branch is live, thick and not flexing.
3. **Door-frame bar** (pull-up bar), *same for strict steps*: no kipping or explosive work.
4. **No hang point at all: Australian Row ladder instead** (low bar / broomstick + 2 chairs / table), *different movement, easier*. Heria's rule (`4Bn3ERXEK-k`): 15-20 good Australian rows, and one pull-up should be in reach. Mark as "substitute", not "same".
- Active Hang with no bar: **no safe alternative** for grip and shoulder hang work; use the substitute.
- Chest-to-Bar, Archer, One-Arm Negative, One-Arm Pull-Up: **no safe alternative without a hang point.**

### Dip

**Bench Dip [bench] / Bench Dip Feet Raised**
1. **Chair Dip** (chair against a wall), *same*: hands on the seat edge, chair backed against a wall / walk the feet out, lower to about 90 degrees at the elbow, press up / chair not braced, so it tips forward. (`RPkTy8emOgQ`: secure, against a wall, sturdy.)
2. **Couch-Frame or Stair Dip** (couch frame or bottom stair), *same*: hands on the firm edge / same / sinking into a soft cushion.
3. Feet Raised version: feet on a second chair or box, *same as the original*.

**Dip Support Hold / Negative Dip / Dip [dipbars]**
1. **Two-Chair Dip** (2 sturdy chairs, backs or seats facing in, set shoulder-width apart), *about the same* (chair height can limit depth): one hand per chair, legs bent behind you / lower as deep as the chairs allow, press up / chairs not loaded straight down, so they tip inward. Heria's "chair dips" (`J7R8whSA17Q`) give bench dips as the easier version.
2. **Parallettes Support Hold** (parallettes), *same for the hold step only*: no full dip depth.
3. **Kitchen-Counter Corner Dip** (an L-shaped counter corner) [general practice], *same*: only on a fixed, stone or solidly built counter.

**Straight-Bar Dip [bar]**
1. **Low bar / park bar at chest height** (low bar), *same*.
2. **Band-Assisted Straight-Bar Dip** (bar + band), *easier* (frames in `hQ2lHMP4kco`).
3. **No safe alternative** without a fixed straight bar. Keep training parallel-bar dips (the broomstick setup is not strong enough for dips).

**Ring Dip [rings]**
1. **No equal alternative.** Rings add instability that nothing else copies (`TRZhw7t7Qgo`). Stay on bar dips, or do **Weighted Dip** for strength.

**Weighted Dip**: backpack of books instead of a vest, *same* (`J7R8whSA17Q`, `2NmjC1NMlwQ`).

### Row

**Incline Row / Inverted Row / Wide-Grip Row / Feet-Elevated Row [lowbar]**
1. **Broomstick Row** (broomstick + 2 chairs), *same*: pole across two weighted chairs, lie under it / pull your chest to the pole, lower slowly / chairs not weighted, so they shift. (`4Bn3ERXEK-k`)
2. **Two-Chair Body Row** (2 sturdy chairs), *easier* (knees bent): lie between two chairs gripping their edges / row your chest up, lower slowly / chairs tipping inward (brace them). (`QqZV3JEoOlc`)
3. **Table Row** (sturdy table) [general practice], *same*: lie under the long edge, grip near a leg / pull your chest to the edge / table lifting at the far end. Test first.
4. **Rings Row** (rings), *same or harder*: walk your feet forward to make it harder.
5. **Towel Door Row** (door + towel) [general practice], *easier*: towel shut in a door that closes toward you / lean back, row in / door opening toward you, which is dangerous.
- Feet-Elevated Row: put the feet on a chair or box. With the broomstick setup only if the chairs are heavily weighted.

**Archer Row [rings]**
1. **Archer Australian Row on a low bar** (low bar or broomstick), *same*: wide grip / pull toward one hand while the other arm stays straight / turning it into a two-arm row. (`4Bn3ERXEK-k` does it on a fixed bar.)
2. **Typewriter Row / One-Arm Row Hold** (low bar), *same or harder* (`4Bn3ERXEK-k`).

**Tuck Front-Lever Row / Adv Tuck FL Row [bar]**
1. **Rings** (rings), *same*.
2. **Band-Assisted** (bar + band), *easier* (`SeIfKANM8rQ`, `hQ2lHMP4kco`).
3. **No safe alternative** without a hang point.

### Legs

**Box Sit-to-Stand / Box Pistol [box]**
1. **Chair / Couch** (chair or couch), *same*: higher = easier (`0rh5HCNU_Iw`, `g30vNrUdOZg`, `flQVCWBuVgk`) / sit back with control, stand on one leg / dropping onto the seat.
2. **Stairs** (bottom step), *harder* (lower).

**Assisted Pistol [pole]**
1. **Door-Frame or Table-Edge Hold** (door frame, sturdy table), *same*: hold at hip height beside you (`flQVCWBuVgk` lists a bar or a table).
2. **Band or Ring Hold** (band or one ring from an anchor), *same*: hold it in the opposite hand (`FxkULisPEo4`).
3. **Wall-Side Assist** (wall), *harder*: hand flat on the wall, full depth (`0rh5HCNU_Iw` says it is harder than sitting to a box).
4. **Towel in a closed door** [general practice], *same*: door must close toward you.

### Core / hanging core

**Knee Raise / Leg Raise / Hanging L-Sit [bar]**
1. **Two-Chair Knee Raise / Leg Raise / L-Sit** (2 chairs), *about the same core load, easier grip*: chairs turned sideways, hands on the seat edges, arms straight / raise the knees or legs and hold / shrugging the shoulders up (push down instead). (`1jjNjn8B9Qw`, `flQVCWBuVgk`)
2. **Parallettes or dip bars** (parallettes / dip bars), *same*.
3. **Floor Lying Leg Raise** (floor), *easier* (`1jjNjn8B9Qw` gives it as the regression).

**Toes-to-Bar / Windshield Wipers / Around-the-World [bar]**
1. **Rings** (rings), *same*.
2. **No safe equivalent without a hang point.** Lying windshield wipers on the floor are a different, easier exercise; offer them only as a "substitute".

### L-sit

**Tuck L-Sit / One-Leg L-Sit / L-Sit / Tucked V-Sit / V-Sit [parallettes]**
1. **Two Chairs or Two Boxes** (2 chairs or boxes), *easier*: more leg room and less hip-flexor demand (`Qv6j5gZyBQ8`, `Qcde45O8lzo`) / press down, shoulders down, lift / one chair only, which gives less room.
2. **Dip Bars** (dip bars), *easier* (`Qcde45O8lzo`: dip bars are easier than box or floor).
3. **Stack of Books** (two even stacks), *same*: a few cm off the floor like parallettes (`Qcde45O8lzo`) / same / uneven stacks.
4. **Floor** (floor), *harder*: this is the "Floor L-Sit" step.

**Manna [parallettes]**
1. **Floor Manna** (floor), *harder*.
2. Books: **not advised** (the backward lean shifts the load onto the stack edges). Chairs: not advised. **No safe alternative** other than the floor.

### Handstand / HSPU (floor, box, wall)
- Box-elevated pike push-up: **couch or stairs**, *same*; higher = harder for pike (`g30vNrUdOZg`). Chair only against a wall.
- Wall steps: any clear load-bearing wall. **No safe alternative** for wall steps without a wall (outside: a solid fence or building wall).

### Muscle-up (all bar)

**Hollow-Arch Swing / Explosive Pull-Up / Negative MU / Bar MU / Strict MU [bar]**
1. **Park or high bar** only. A door-frame bar is **not safe** for swings, explosive pulls or muscle-ups.
2. Ring muscle-up is a different technique (false grip), so it is **not an equivalent**.
3. **No safe alternative** without a fixed high bar.

**Jumping MU [lowbar]**
1. **Box under a high bar** (box + high bar), *same*: Heria's own setup (`2NmjC1NMlwQ`) / stand on the box, jump-pull, lean over the bar, press out / jumping without leaning over the bar.
2. **Low bar, standing jump** (low bar), *same*.

### Front lever [bar]
1. **Rings** (rings), *same*.
2. **Band-Assisted** (bar + band), *easier* (section 4).
3. **Floor prep only: Tucked Dragon Press Hold** (floor), *different, easier*: shoulders on the floor, raise the tucked hips (`QqZV3JEoOlc`). Not a replacement.
4. **No safe alternative** without a hang point.

### Back lever [rings]
1. **Pull-Up Bar** (pull-up bar), *same*: Heria teaches the whole ladder on a bar (`FZZqbeZti84`) / overhand grip, shoulder width, very tight grip / loose grip when turning over.
2. **No safe alternative** without a hang point. German hang and skin the cat put a heavy load on the shoulders; never do them on a door-frame bar.

### Planche
- Scap Push-Up / Lean [floor]: no gear needed.
- **Tuck / Adv Tuck / Straddle / Full [parallettes]**
  1. **Two Chairs** (2 equal chairs, braced), *same*: Heria uses two chairs to lower from L-sit into tuck planche (`OmKfROtB45Q`) / straight arms, lean, lift / chairs at different heights.
  2. **Floor, flat hands** (floor), *harder* (`OmKfROtB45Q`): more wrist strain; warm the wrists up.
  3. **Band-Assisted** (band + high bar), *easier* (section 4).
  4. Books: **not advised** for planche (forward lean plus swings).

### Human flag
- Side Plank / Side Plank Raise [floor]: no gear. Harder floor version: **feet-elevated high side-plank raise** (`TF9XhvYh_m8`).
- **Vertical Flag / Tuck / Straddle / Full [pole]**
  1. **Wall bars** (wall bars), *same*.
  2. **Solid fixed post** (steel sign post or squat-rack upright bolted down), *same*: only if it does not flex.
  3. **No safe alternative** without a fixed vertical pole. Door frames, tree trunks you cannot grip, and loose racks are not safe.

### Dragon flag [bench]
1. **Floor + Fixed Vertical Anchor** (floor + pole, wall bars or heavy fixed furniture), *same*: THENX's own setup (`XQ28-rSbiP8`, frames) / lie on the floor, grip the pole behind your head, upper back and traps are the only contact / lift and lower as one straight line / bending at the hips.
2. **Bench + grip the bench behind the head** (bench), *same*.
3. **No safe alternative** if there is nothing fixed to grip. Never use a chair or a loose table.

## 4. Band-assisted steps

**Band rules from the videos:**
- **Pull-ups:** the band goes on the bar, with the knee or foot in the loop. For high pulls it goes under the glutes, which is the seated band L-sit pull-up (`ereWycVxlwo`). A thicker or tighter band gives more help. Heria warns not to get too used to the band (`XeErfmGSwfE`).
- **Front lever:** grip the band ends between your palms and the bar, with the band across the waist just above the glutes, as tight as possible. Over time, loosen it, then switch to a thinner band (`ereWycVxlwo`, `SeIfKANM8rQ`).
- **Planche:** hang the band from a high bar so it pulls **straight up** at the **waist** (mark the floor under it). Placed under the knees it helps too much. If it is set too far back it tips you forward; too far forward, it pulls you back (`ereWycVxlwo`).
- **Dips / straight-bar dips:** band across the bars, with the knees in it (`hQ2lHMP4kco`, frames).
- **Making a move harder:** a band anchored under the parallettes and across the lower back adds resistance to planche holds and presses. A band across the back adds resistance to push-ups (`ereWycVxlwo`, `hQ2lHMP4kco`).

**How the band is progressed:**
- Within one step, go from **thicker to thinner**. For the front lever, first loosen the wrap, then thin the band.
- Rule for the app: move to the next thinner band when the target reps or hold time are reached with perfect form. After the thinnest band, test the move without a band.
- Across planche steps, THENX gives the **thinnest band for tuck, a medium band for advanced tuck, a thicker band for straddle and the thickest for full**. Longer levers need more help, so a person may move back up to a thicker band when they move up a step. The app must not treat "thicker band" as a regression of the step.
- Pick a band that still allows perfect form (`x0Yx9qbZuX8`).

## 6. Where the existing ladders and the videos disagree

1. **Dragon flag gear is wrong.** The app lists a bench; THENX does it on the floor gripping a vertical pole (`XQ28-rSbiP8`). The ladder is also missing **Switching Legs** and **Single-Leg Dragon Flag Raise** between Negative and Full. THENX order: hip raise, negative, switching legs, single-leg raise, flutter kick, full. Entry requirements: 10 s L-sit, 5 upside-down deadlifts, 10 pull-ups.
2. **Back lever gear is wrong.** The app lists rings; the THENX ladder runs entirely on a pull-up bar (`FZZqbeZti84`). The app is missing the **Upside-Down (Inverted) Hold** (hold 20 s, after tuck) and the **Single-Leg Back Lever Negative** (before the two-leg negative). Entry requirements: 20 s hang, 15 hanging knee raises, 10 pull-ups, 10 s hanging L-sit. German hang does not appear in the video. Keep it, but it is not sourced.
3. **Human flag: "Vertical Flag" is not a step in either video.** Both use a **Pole Side Plank (feet on the floor)**: bottom arm pushing, top arm pulling, with the head directly under the top hand. Then the same with one leg raised, then flag bounces, straddle flag raises, **Flag Negative** and flag raises (`bG0h7bSfxQI`, `TF9XhvYh_m8`). Suggest replacing Vertical Flag with Pole Side Plank and adding Flag Negative before Full.
4. **Jumping muscle-up gear.** The app lists a low bar; Heria uses a **box under a normal high bar** (`2NmjC1NMlwQ`). Also add a hard gate before muscle-up skill work: **20 pull-ups, 20 straight-bar dips, 20 s L-sit, 10 toes-to-bar**.
5. **L-sit difficulty depends on the surface.** Chairs or boxes are easiest, then parallettes or books, then the floor (hardest). Dip bars are easier than box or floor (`Qcde45O8lzo`). The app's Floor L-Sit must stay after L-Sit. When someone without parallettes uses chairs, their step is easier than the listed one; log it that way.
6. **Pull ladder skips the row bridge.** Heria's first-pull-up path goes active hang (45 s), scapular shrugs (x10), **Australian rows (10, at lower and lower angles, with three grip widths)**, jump negatives, then pull-up (`XeErfmGSwfE`). The app goes straight from Scapula Pull-Up to Negative. Suggest a gate: Inverted Row x10 before Negative Pull-Up. Rule of thumb: 15-20 rows ≈ 1 pull-up (`4Bn3ERXEK-k`).
7. **Archer Row does not need rings.** It is done on a fixed low bar in `4Bn3ERXEK-k`; change the gear to low bar (rings optional).
8. **Planche ladder is missing bridge steps.** The 10-step plan includes **Planche Lean Hold** (10 s, hands at the waistline), **Reverse Leg Raise** on two chairs (15 reps, 15 s hold) and **L-Sit to Tuck Planche** (7-10 reps) before the Tuck Planche hold. Entry requirements: 10 s L-sit, 25 push-ups, 10 pull-ups (`OmKfROtB45Q`). Tuck planche works on chairs, so parallettes are not required. Flat hands on the floor are harder than parallettes.
9. **Front lever ladder differs from Heria's.** Heria uses toes-to-bar, tuck, advanced tuck, **upside-down deadlift / upside-down hold**, single-leg negatives, two-leg negatives, **half-lay** (knees bent, hips open), then full (`5g8-sj-8snc`, `SeIfKANM8rQ`). The app has no inverted step before Negative. Suggest adding Upside-Down Hold before Negative. Straddle is fine to keep (common elsewhere), but half-lay is Heria's step at that position. Entry requirements: 10-15 pull-ups, 20 s hanging L-sit, 15 hanging leg raises, 5-10 L-sit pull-ups.
10. **Assisted pistol gear.** The app lists a pole; any fixed support works (bar, table, band, ring) (`flQVCWBuVgk`, `FxkULisPEo4`). Wall-side assistance is harder than sitting to a box (`0rh5HCNU_Iw`). The legs order otherwise matches Heria's: box squat, air squat, jump squat, box pistol, assisted pistol, pistol.
11. **Ring Dip after Straight-Bar Dip is fine.** THENX says ring dips are much harder than bar dips (`TRZhw7t7Qgo`). No change.

No contradiction found for the push, core, handstand or HSPU ladders. The videos did not cover hollow-arch swings, the shrimp squat, Manna or around-the-world in detail, so those steps are unchecked by the sources.
